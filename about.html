About eNVision

A story about seeing the world through a better lens.

Our Story

eNVision started in 2017 as a small optical boutique in the heart of Jakarta. Founded with the belief that eyewear should be more than just a medical device, we set out to create a space where vision care meets contemporary design.

What started as a single storefront quickly grew into a destination for design-conscious customers seeking premium frames and expert care. By 2020, eNVision expanded into online retail, bringing our curated collections to customers across Southeast Asia.

Today, eNVision serves thousands of customers worldwide and continues to grow — but our commitment to quality and personal service has never changed.

Our Philosophy

We believe that the right pair of glasses can transform how you see the world — and how the world sees you. At eNVision, every product is selected and crafted with intention.

We source only materials that meet our standards: Italian acetate, Japanese titanium, and precision-cut lenses from certified suppliers. We work with independent designers and in-house craftspeople to ensure every frame is both beautiful and built to last.

Our philosophy is simple: great eyewear is functional art. We design for people who care about both.
